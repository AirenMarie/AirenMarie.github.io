About Me

Hello! I'm Airen Stringer, and I'm a student at Code:You, working towards a career in Web Development.

So, what made me decide to study at Code:You?

After years of working at UPS, I decided I wanted to do something different with my life, something more fulfilling and lucrative, which would allow me to express myself creatively while making something functional and beneficial to the community around me.

Why Web Development?

I have always been interested in how Web pages work, ever since the days when platforms Angelfire and Geocities were popular. Those platforms gave users an opportunity to be creative and make their own unique spaces online.

With Code:You, I'm learning the necessary skills that will help me to not only create those interesting online spaces, but also to make them into responsive, functional applications that are useful to everyone.
